Install App Guide

For iPhone & iPad (Safari):

  1. Tap Safari's Share button ⎋ / ⍎ at the bottom of your screen.
  2. Scroll down the menu and tap "Add to Home Screen" .
  3. Tap "Add" in the top-right corner to place the app on your home screen.

For Android Devices (Chrome):

  1. Tap Chrome's Menu icon ⋮ in the top-right corner.
  2. Select "Install app" or "Add to Home screen".
  3. Tap "Install" in the popup prompt to confirm.

For PC & Mac (Chrome / Edge):

  1. Look at the right side of your browser's Address Bar at the top.
  2. Click the Install icon ⤓ or ⊕.
  3. Click "Install" to launch the portal as a standalone app.
